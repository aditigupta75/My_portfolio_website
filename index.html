Case study — flagship project
Wearable Multi-Sensor Radar Health-Monitoring System
Custom PCB (EasyEDA) · ESP32/FreeRTOS · RD-03E 24 GHz mmWave Radar · CNN · MQTT → MongoDB

Problem

Contact biosensors alone (heart-rate, GSR, temperature, motion) become unreliable the moment they're dislodged during sleep — the exact scenario continuous monitoring needs to survive.

Approach

Designed a 2-layer custom PCB fusing MAX30102, GSR, TMP117, and BNO055 contact sensors with a non-contact 24 GHz mmWave radar module. ESP32/FreeRTOS firmware handles real-time acquisition and switches adaptively between contact and contactless modes. A CNN classifies sleep/health state from the fused stream; MQTT feeds a MongoDB backend for storage.

Result

An end-to-end, patent-oriented prototype spanning PCB, firmware, ML, and cloud. The classification pipeline reached ~80% accuracy in preliminary, informal testing — not yet formally validated on a defined test set.
